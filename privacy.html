Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

This policy covers felipecarrillo100.github.io and the project demo pages linked from it (including, among others, the base62str demo). Each of those demos is a separate open-source project, but they share this one policy and this one advertising setup, since none of them collect data of their own beyond what's described here.

What this site collects

Nothing personal, and nothing on a server. Every page here — the hub site and the linked project demos — is a static site with no accounts, no sign-in, and no backend. The only browser storage used is a single localStorage entry on this hub site to remember your light/dark theme choice; it never leaves your browser and isn't sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site and its linked demos show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites, and may use non-personalized or personalized advertising depending on your region and your consent choices.

You can learn more about how Google uses this information, and control your ad settings, here:

Your consent choices

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent message (via a Google-certified consent management platform) before any personalized ad cookies are set, with clear options to consent, decline, or manage choices in detail. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site and reloading the page.

Children's privacy

These sites are not directed at children under 13, and no data is knowingly collected from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in how this site or its advertising works. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the latest revision.
